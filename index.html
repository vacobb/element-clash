FIELD GUIDE

Your first battle.

Desktop

WASD or arrows move. Nearby visible opponents are targeted automatically. Click an opponent to lock; click empty space to clear. Press 1–6 or click a suggestion to cast. Hold Spacebar or BASIC ATTACK for basic shots. E opens the spellbook.

Phone

Left stick moves. Tap a suggestion to cast immediately. Tap an opponent to lock; tap empty space for automatic targeting. Hold BASIC ATTACK for basic shots. Movement and casting support simultaneous touches.

Gather → craft → cast

Walk over ingredients to collect them. Six suggestions match your inventory, with defense always in slot 6. Attacks target a visible opponent in range; healing and shields affect you. Dashes follow your movement direction. A ring marks the current target; gold indicates a lock.

Survive the storm

100 health. Potions share a 10-second cooldown; large spells share a 4-second cooldown. Shields expire. After 90 seconds on standard pace, the boundary contracts. Last survivor wins. Simultaneous final deaths are a draw.

Eliminated wizards drop up to four ingredients and spectate. You have 30 seconds to reconnect; your character remains vulnerable. Teams share victory with no friendly fire. Skirmish lasts three minutes, with four-second respawns and eliminations as the score.
